# Project Overview

## Project Name
Demo E-Commerce Project

## Purpose
To build an e-commerce platform for client hunting purposes, where sellers and customers can easily navigate the website and make purchases. Customers can search products from the product list, add items to the cart, and check out easily using the best payment gateway.

**Payment Gateway:** SSLCommerz

The seller (our client) will be provided with a useful dashboard to:
- Manage products
- Manage orders
- Manage users of the platform
- View overall revenue and business statistics
- Manage the overall shop, shop products, and orders

## Problem Statement
Sellers, particularly SMEs (Small and Medium Enterprises) in Bangladesh, currently face issues in:
- Managing their shop
- Selling products and listing them properly
- Tracking their customers
- Tracking their orders

## Target Users
People who want to purchase products online:
- Without any hassle
- With a beautiful UI/UX
- With easy navigation across the application
- With simple, easy-to-follow user flows/workflows

## Business Goals
Boost the client's (seller's) business performance, including:
- Up to 3x increase in business performance
- Increased order counts
- Better product management
- Overall business growth

## Expected Outcome
Build a website that provides:
- An intuitive UI
- Effective dashboard management features
